# Sai Institutional Books Compiler

Status: source pinned and compiler mechanics implemented; book text access is not
yet active on this machine. No Institutional Books volume is training-ready.

automatically admitted corpus.

## Rights and access boundary

The pinned release describes the collection as public-domain books. Its
Early-Access Terms independently limit the Service to noncommercial use,
prohibit redistribution, require attribution, and tell commercial actors or
commercial AI-training users to contact IDI first. Those terms are part of the
source identity and may change in later revisions.

Public-domain status of a work and permission to use this particular packaged
Service are different questions. A HathiTrust `pd` code is preserved as external
evidence; Hermes cannot turn it into a new legal conclusion. No text is packed
until the intended Sai use is compatible with the pinned terms and the exact
volume's evidence remains acceptable.

The text repositories are gated. The local Hugging Face CLI was not authenticated
when this source was pinned on 2026-08-23. Metadata can be inventoried without
accepting text access, but book-agent execution requires the account holder to
accept the current terms and authenticate. Dataset bytes or derivatives will not
be committed or redistributed through GitHub.

## Archive quality is not automatic training quality

Harvard Library provenance, public-domain selection, OCR refinement, collection
deduplication, and enriched-text processing make this an unusually valuable
reservoir. They do not prove that every volume is accurate, balanced, readable,
pedagogically useful, or culturally safe. The dataset card itself distinguishes
generated fields and warns that historical collections can contain outdated,
harmful, or inaccurate material.

Every volume therefore retains separate judgments for:

- overall quality and OCR quality;
- literary and historical value;
- knowledge density and factual reliability;
- bibliographic ambiguity and duplicate editions;
- historical context, outdated claims, and cultural provenance;
- linguistic, conceptual, and reasoning complexity.

## Exact book record

Archive-supplied evidence is immutable:

- `source_id` is `barcode_src`;
- title, author, publication dates, source and detected language;
- page/token counts and independent OCR scores;
- subjects, genre/form notes, library identifiers, and duplicate barcodes;
- HathiTrust rights code, reason, URL, and last-check date;
- dataset revisions, card hashes, metadata-row hash, enriched-row hash, and text
  field.

Hermes may propose, but never authoritatively assert:

- `work_id_candidate` and `edition_id_candidate`;
- normalized author and author-death candidate;
- normalized publication date, original language, translator, and translation
  date candidates;
- domains, subdomains, genre, style, period, culture, and geography;
- quality scores, three independent complexity axes, concepts, prerequisites,
  and evidence-backed graph edges;
- translation route, confidence, derived representations, and duplicate work
  candidates.

The compiler receipt labels these as candidates and copies rights evidence from
the archive envelope rather than from model output. Raw source and every judgment
remain `training_ready=false`.

## Representative reading and full-book stages

The first metadata pass does not send 217B tokens through Hermes. For each
selected volume, `sai.data.institutional_books` binds a deterministic
beginning/middle/end excerpt of at most 192 KiB. This supports efficient
classification while making the partial-reading boundary explicit.

A retained volume then advances to a separate full-book compiler stage that can
split chapters, detect front/back-matter contamination, refine work/edition
clusters, and construct representations. An excerpt judgment cannot certify all
claims or all OCR pages in a book.

## Translation policy

Language is never a rejection reason. Retained non-English books preserve the
original-language source anchor and route knowledge to English.

For technical, scientific, historical, and expository material, Hermes may
produce a faithful English rendering plus source-grounded explanations.

For literature, poetry, and drama:

1. search first for a reputable public-domain human English translation of the
   same work and edition family;
2. if one exists, preserve and use it with its translator and publication
   lineage;
3. otherwise generate both a literal translation and a separate literary
   translation;
4. label both generated forms as synthetic translations and preserve the
   original-language anchor;
5. reject a single generic-prose translation route.

The literal form protects semantic auditability. The literary form attempts to
carry rhythm, ambiguity, syntax, voice, imagery, and register. Neither is
misrepresented as a historical human translation.

## Concept graph and spiral curriculum

A single `difficulty=7` cannot distinguish ornate prose from prerequisite-dense
thermodynamics. Book judgments therefore expose:

- `linguistic_complexity`;
- `conceptual_complexity`;
- `reasoning_complexity`;
- explicit prerequisite-to-dependent edges with confidence and an exact source
  quote;
- a curriculum band based on prerequisite readiness rather than a scalar mean.

`sai.data.spiral_curriculum` freezes this prospective rehearsal schedule:

| Phase | Basic | Intermediate | Advanced | Expert |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Early | 65% | 25% | 8% | 2% |
| Foundation | 40% | 40% | 15% | 5% |
| Growth | 20% | 40% | 30% | 10% |
| Advanced | 10% | 25% | 40% | 25% |
| Annealing | 10% | 20% | 35% | 35% |

Basics never disappear. A concept enters only when its graph prerequisites have
been previously represented or are deliberately rehearsed in that phase. The
curriculum is a warmup and pacing policy, not an irreversible easy-then-hard
sort; later mixed sampling and continued foundational rehearsal are mandatory.

This design is consistent with the 2026 EACL systematic curriculum study, which
reported faster early/mid convergence and retained gains when curriculum was
used as warmup before mixed sampling. It also directly measures retention because
pretraining forgetting has been observed in ACL and TACL studies.

## Executable boundary

- `sai.data.institutional_books` pins sources and builds exact joined candidates.
- `sai.data.institutional_books_selection` creates the duplicate-safe,
  coverage-first metadata review queue.
- `sai.data.book_compiler_labeling` defines the book judgment and translation
  contract.
- `sai.data.nous_book_compiler_worker` runs create-only persistent Hermes shards.
- `sai.data.spiral_curriculum` creates and validates the phase allocation policy.

The general FineWeb compiler continues independently. Book execution begins only
after text-gate authentication; it will use a separate output root and will not
overwrite or reinterpret prior compiler receipts.
